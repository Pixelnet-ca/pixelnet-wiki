## Video

### Lava RGB 2.0

RGB, S-Video and clean composite straight from the PPU, 8 palettes and in-game reset. Comes with a new power/A/V module.

:material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it** · [:material-book-open-variant: Guide](../../guides/nes-lava-rgb.md)

### NESRGB

Board that sits under the PPU and adds RGB with selectable palettes. Often paired with a multi-out so you can use SNES cables.

### Hi-Def NES

Digital HDMI output (up to 1080p) with palettes, scanlines and an on-screen menu. The sharpest option.

### AV mod (top-loader, Famicom)

Adds the composite (and sometimes S-Video) output that the NES-101 and the original Famicom are missing.

## Audio

### Expansion audio

Wires the expansion port so Famicom expansion sound chips (VRC6, etc.) play through the NES, with a flash cart or adapter.

## Region & frequency

### 10NES disable (CIC)

Cuts one pin on the lockout chip. Ends the boot-blinking caused by the chip and lets other-region carts run.

## Maintenance & reliability

### 72-pin connector

Refurbishing or replacing the cartridge connector: the main cure for the blinking grey screen.
