### Internal HDMI

Internal kits that tap the digital video for a cleaner HDMI picture than HDMI adapters.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r3">★★★☆☆</span> **Good, with caveats** · Cleaner than adapters, but costly and a delicate install. Component is enough for most people.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d4">■■■■</span> **Expert** · Desoldering large chips or lifting chip pins, board rework. Good desoldering tools needed.
</div>

## Softmod & homebrew

### Softmod (Homebrew Channel)

Installs the Homebrew Channel without opening the console: emulators, USB loading and Nintendont for GameCube games.
