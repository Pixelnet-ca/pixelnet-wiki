## Video

Not a mod, but the best stock picture: 480p over component, far better than the stock composite.

### Retro G.E.M.

Internal HDMI kit that taps the console's digital video. Basic version (720p, great with an external scaler) or Shiny version (full built-in scaling). With a PixelFX Morph 4K, adds Game-ID and per-game settings.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · The cleanest Wii picture, but component cables are already good for most people.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.
</div>

### Internal HDMI

Other internal kits that tap the digital video for a cleaner HDMI picture than HDMI adapters.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r3">★★★☆☆</span> **Good, with caveats** · Cleaner than adapters, but costly and a delicate install. Component is enough for most people.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d4">■■■■</span> **Expert** · Desoldering large chips or lifting chip pins, board rework. Good desoldering tools needed.
</div>

## Softmod & homebrew

### Softmod (Homebrew Channel)

Installs the Homebrew Channel without opening the console: emulators, USB loading and Nintendont for GameCube games.
