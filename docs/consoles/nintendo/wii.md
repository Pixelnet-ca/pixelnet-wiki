*2006–2013* · Plays GameCube games too (RVL-001). Most Wii mods are software only.

| Model | Note |

| **RVL-101 / RVL-201** | Family / Mini: no GameCube support |

## Video

### Component cable

Not a mod, but the best stock picture: 480p over component, far better than the stock composite.

:material-power-plug: No install

### Internal HDMI

Internal kits that tap the digital video for a cleaner HDMI picture than HDMI adapters.

## Softmod & homebrew

### Softmod (Homebrew Channel)

Installs the Homebrew Channel without opening the console: emulators, USB loading and Nintendont for GameCube games.

:material-power-plug: No install

## Upgrades & features

### Portable build (trim)

Hobbyist project: the motherboard is cut down and rebuilt into a handheld.
