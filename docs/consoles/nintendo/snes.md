# Super Nintendo / Super Famicom

*1990–1999* · Outputs RGB stock, but 2-chip boards have a soft, ghosty picture. Video cleanup and region mods are the classics.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **SNS-001** | Original (2-chip or 1CHIP) |
| **SNS-101** | SNES Jr / Mini: no RGB stock |
| **SHVC-001** | Super Famicom (Japan) |

## Video

### CleanRGB + EdgeBuster (RetroSix)

Rebuilds the RGB signal and removes ghosting and blurry pixel edges. Huge gain on 2-chip boards.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r3">★★★☆☆</span> **Good, with caveats** · Many happy owners and a clean DC design. Some forum users report jitter with the EdgeBuster on certain 2-chip boards; RetroSix advises pairing it with the CleanRGB.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.
</div>

### SNES Edge-Enhancer

2-chip SNES: recovers the sharpness lost in the video circuit, close to 1CHIP quality. Improves RGB, S-Video and composite. A recap is strongly recommended first.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Reviewers (My Life in Gaming, RetroRGB) found it brings 2-chip consoles close to 1CHIP sharpness, on RGB, S-Video and composite. Often out of stock.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.

### SNESRGB (bypass)

RGB bypass amplifier: cleaner RGB on 1CHIP consoles, and brings RGB back on the SNES Jr. A 2-chip version is paired with the Edge-Enhancer.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · Well-regarded bypass amp, the usual fix for the SNES Jr's missing RGB. Requires removing components.

## Region & frequency

### SuperCIC

Replaces the lockout chip: play any region and switch 50/60 Hz from the reset button, with a status LED.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · The best SNES region mod, with full import compatibility. Botched installs are common: take your time.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.
</div>

### Cartridge slot trim

Removes the plastic tabs so Super Famicom carts fit a North American SNES.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · Free and easy, but permanent: the plastic tabs don't come back.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d1">■□□□</span> **Easy** · Screwdriver (often a game bit), drop-in parts, no soldering.
</div>

## Power & battery

### Switching regulator

Replaces the hot 7805 linear regulator with a modern drop-in: less heat, more stable power.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · Cheap, simple and cuts the heat inside the console.
