# Super Nintendo / Super Famicom

*1990–1999* · Outputs RGB stock, but 2-chip boards have a soft, ghosty picture. Video cleanup and region mods are the classics.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **SNS-001** | Original (2-chip or 1CHIP) |
| **SNS-101** | SNES Jr / Mini: no RGB stock |
| **SHVC-001** | Super Famicom (Japan) |

**Legend:** :material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it** · :material-book-open-variant: **Guide** on this wiki · :material-power-plug: **No install** (cart, adapter or software)

## Video

### CleanRGB + EdgeBuster (RetroSix)

Rebuilds the RGB signal and removes ghosting and blurry pixel edges. Huge gain on 2-chip boards.

:material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it** · [:material-book-open-variant: Guide](../../guides/snes-cleanrgb-edgebuster.md)

### SNES Jr RGB restore

Adds an RGB amplifier to the SNS-101, which had RGB removed at the factory.

## Games & storage

### FXPak Pro (SD2SNES)

Flash cartridge with support for enhancement chips (Super FX, SA-1…) and save states.

:material-power-plug: No install

## Region & frequency

### SuperCIC

Replaces the lockout chip: play any region and switch 50/60 Hz from the reset button, with a status LED.

### Cartridge slot trim

Removes the plastic tabs so Super Famicom carts fit a North American SNES.

## Upgrades & features

### CPU overclock

Raises the CPU clock to reduce slowdown in some games. Compatibility varies by game.

## Power & battery

### Switching regulator

Replaces the hot 7805 linear regulator with a modern drop-in: less heat, more stable power.
