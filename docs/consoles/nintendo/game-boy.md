# Game Boy / Game Boy Pocket

*1989–1998* · The original green screen has no backlight. A backlit IPS screen and a rechargeable battery change everything.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **DMG-01** | Original Game Boy |
| **MGB-001** | Game Boy Pocket |

**Legend:** :material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it** · :material-book-open-variant: **Guide** on this wiki · :material-power-plug: **No install** (cart, adapter or software)

## Screen

### IPS screen kit

Bright backlit IPS screen with brightness levels and colour palettes. Some kits need a trimmed or new shell.

### Backlight + bivert

Older approach: a backlight behind the stock LCD, with a bivert chip to restore contrast.

## Audio

### Pro Sound

Takes audio straight from the CPU for a clean line output. A favourite of chiptune musicians.

### Speaker / amp upgrade

A louder, clearer speaker and amplifier.

## Power & battery

### USB-C rechargeable battery

Li-ion battery kit charged by USB-C, instead of AA batteries.

## Maintenance & reliability

### Display ribbon fix

Repairs missing vertical lines on the stock screen (ribbon cable on the DMG).

## Shell & buttons

### Shell, buttons and glass lens

New shell, coloured buttons and a glass screen lens.
