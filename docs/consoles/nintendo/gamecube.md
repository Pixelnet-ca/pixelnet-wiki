# GameCube

*2001–2007* · Excellent picture in 480p. Mods focus on HDMI and loading games from an SD card.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **DOL-001** | Has the Digital AV port |
| **DOL-101** | No Digital AV port |

**Legend:** :material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it** · :material-book-open-variant: **Guide** on this wiki · :material-power-plug: **No install** (cart, adapter or software) · :material-cart: **Buy** from the official source

## Video

### GCVideo HDMI

Open-source firmware that turns the digital video into clean HDMI. Plug-in on DOL-001 (Digital AV port), internal install on DOL-101.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · The go-to GameCube HDMI solution. Plug-in on the DOL-001; on the DOL-101 the internal install is advanced soldering.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d1">■□□□</span> **Easy** · Screwdriver (often a game bit), drop-in parts, no soldering.
</div>

[:material-cart: Buy](https://github.com/ikorb/gcvideo){ target=_blank }

## Games & storage

### GC Loader

ODE that replaces the disc drive: games load from an SD card.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Solder-free and the best compatibility and loading speed, since it uses the drive's own fast bus.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d1">■□□□</span> **Easy** · Screwdriver (often a game bit), drop-in parts, no soldering.
</div>

### SD2SP2

SD card adapter in Serial Port 2 (DOL-001). Used with Swiss to load games and homebrew.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · Cheap and works well with Swiss. A few games stutter in videos, and you still need a way to boot Swiss.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d0">□□□□</span> **Plug and play** · No tools, nothing to open.
</div>

:material-power-plug: No install

## Softmod & homebrew

### PicoBoot

Small board on the IPL chip that boots Swiss (homebrew) automatically at power-on.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · Cheap and keeps the disc drive. Small solder points; a few users report it stopping working later.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.
</div>

[:material-cart: Buy](https://github.com/webhdx/PicoBoot){ target=_blank }

## Maintenance & reliability

### Drive and laser service

Cleaning, adjusting or replacing the laser when discs stop reading.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r3">★★★☆☆</span> **Good, with caveats** · Worth trying before replacing the drive. A GC Loader is often the more lasting fix.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d2">■■□□</span> **Intermediate** · Basic soldering: a few wires or through-hole parts.
</div>
