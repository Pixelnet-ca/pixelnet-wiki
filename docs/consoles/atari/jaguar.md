## Games & storage

### Jaguar GameDrive

Flash cartridge: cartridge games from an SD card.

:material-power-plug: No install

## Softmod & homebrew

### BJL (Behind Jaguar Lines)

Replacement BIOS for uploading homebrew from a computer.

## Region & frequency

### 50/60 Hz switch (PAL)

Runs PAL consoles at 60 Hz for full speed.

## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap

Replaces the old electrolytic capacitors. Fixes or prevents hum, weak sound, dim picture and power problems.
