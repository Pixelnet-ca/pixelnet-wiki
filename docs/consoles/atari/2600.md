## Video

### 2600RGB

RGB, S-Video and composite plus audio, 6 palettes with auto PAL/NTSC, on-screen menu. Fits every model (harder on the 2600jr).

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · The best 2600 picture, RGB plus excellent S-Video. Some games with unstable line counts upset scalers.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.
</div>

:material-account-hard-hat: by [Tim Worthington](https://etim.net.au){ target=_blank } · [:material-cart: Buy](https://etim.net.au/shop/shop.php){ target=_blank }

### CleanComp

Clean composite video output in place of RF.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · Users report a very nice composite and S-Video picture.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d2">■■□□</span> **Intermediate** · Basic soldering: a few wires or through-hole parts.

## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap

Replaces the old electrolytic capacitors. Fixes or prevents hum, weak sound, dim picture and power problems.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r3">★★★☆☆</span> **Good, with caveats** · Helps with power and picture issues.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d2">■■□□</span> **Intermediate** · Basic soldering: a few wires or through-hole parts.
</div>
