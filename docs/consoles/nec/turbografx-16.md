### RGB mod

## Games & storage

### Turbo EverDrive

Flash HuCard.

:material-power-plug: No install

### Super SD System 3

Plugs into the expansion port and plays CD games from an SD card, no CD drive needed.

:material-power-plug: No install

## Region & frequency

### Region switch

Plays Japanese HuCards on a TurboGrafx-16 (and the other way around).

## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap (Duo: essential)

The TurboDuo's capacitors leak and damage the board: recap it early.

### CD laser service

Laser cleaning, adjustment or replacement.
