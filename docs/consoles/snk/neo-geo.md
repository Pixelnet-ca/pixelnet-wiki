## Upgrades & features

### UniBIOS

Replacement BIOS: region and mode switching, cheats, soundtest and more.

### MVS consolization

Turns an arcade MVS board into a home console with controller ports and A/V output.

## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap

Replaces the old electrolytic capacitors. Fixes or prevents hum, weak sound, dim picture and power problems.

### CD laser service

Laser service for the Neo Geo CD and CDZ.
