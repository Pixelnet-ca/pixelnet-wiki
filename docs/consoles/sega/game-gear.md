## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap (essential)

The capacitors leak on nearly every unit: no sound, dim or blank screen. A recap brings it back.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Almost every Game Gear needs it. Small surface-mount caps.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.
</div>
