## Games & storage

### Satiator

ODE that plugs into the drive connector: games from an SD card, the drive stays in place.

[:material-cart: Buy](https://www.satiator.net){ target=_blank }

### Rhea / Phoebe

ODE that replaces the disc drive and loads from SD (Rhea for Model 1, Phoebe for Model 2).

[:material-cart: Buy](https://www.terraonion.com){ target=_blank }

### Fenrir

Budget ODE that replaces the drive board.

## Region & frequency

### Region Free BIOS

Replacement BIOS to play games from every region.

:material-account-hard-hat: by [RetroSix](https://www.retrosix.co.uk){ target=_blank } · [:material-cart: Buy](https://www.retrosix.co.uk){ target=_blank }

### Region and 50/60 Hz switch

Play Japanese and European games on a North American Saturn, at 50 or 60 Hz.

## Power & battery

### Clock battery

Replace the CR2032 to keep the clock and saves. Easy, no soldering.

### FRAM

Replaces the internal save memory with FRAM so saves survive without the battery.

:material-account-hard-hat: by [RetroSix](https://www.retrosix.co.uk){ target=_blank } · [:material-cart: Buy](https://www.retrosix.co.uk){ target=_blank }

### CleanPower

Modern replacement power board for the Saturn.

:material-account-hard-hat: by [RetroSix](https://www.retrosix.co.uk){ target=_blank } · [:material-cart: Buy](https://www.retrosix.co.uk){ target=_blank }

## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap

Replaces the old electrolytic capacitors. Fixes or prevents hum, weak sound, dim picture and power problems.
