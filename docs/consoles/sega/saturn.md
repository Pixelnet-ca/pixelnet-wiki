## Games & storage

### Satiator

ODE that plugs into the drive connector: games from an SD card, the drive stays in place.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Plug-and-play on the drive connector and removable, so the console stays stock. Active firmware development.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d1">■□□□</span> **Easy** · Screwdriver (often a game bit), drop-in parts, no soldering.
</div>

ODE that replaces the disc drive and loads from SD (Rhea for Model 1, Phoebe for Model 2).

## Region & frequency

## Power & battery

### Clock battery

Replace the CR2032 to keep the clock and saves. Easy, no soldering.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Two minutes, and saves stay put.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d0">□□□□</span> **Plug and play** · No tools, nothing to open.
</div>

### FRAM

Replaces the internal save memory with FRAM so saves survive without the battery.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · Saves survive without the battery.
