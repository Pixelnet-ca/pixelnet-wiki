# Genesis / Mega Drive

*1988–1997* · Outputs RGB stock, but the Model 2 has jailbars and the audio varies by board. Also covers the Sega CD, 32X and Nomad.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **Model 1** | VA0 to VA7, best audio on early boards |
| **Model 2** | VA0 to VA4, jailbars common |
| **Model 3** | Majesco budget model, few mods |
| **Nomad** | Portable Genesis |

**Legend:** :material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it** · :material-book-open-variant: **Guide** on this wiki · :material-power-plug: **No install** (cart, adapter or software)

## Video

### Triple Bypass (3BP)

Model 2: bypasses the stock video encoder. Sharp RGB with no jailbars, plus cleaner composite and S-Video.

:material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it**

## Audio

### Mega Amp

Replaces the audio circuit for cleaner, better-balanced sound on Model 1 and Model 2.

## Games & storage

### Mega EverDrive Pro

Flash cartridge that also plays Sega CD games when a Sega CD is attached.

:material-power-plug: No install

## Region & frequency

### MegaDrive++ / region switch

Change region (language) and 50/60 Hz with a switch or from the controller, with in-game reset.

## Upgrades & features

### Sega CD: BIOS switch

Multi-BIOS chip to play Sega CD games from every region.

### Sega CD / 32X: recap and drive service

Recapping and laser/drive service bring add-ons back to life.

### Nomad: screen and power

Replacement LCD kits, recap and a modern battery pack for the portable Genesis.

## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap

Replaces the old electrolytic capacitors. Fixes or prevents hum, weak sound, dim picture and power problems.
