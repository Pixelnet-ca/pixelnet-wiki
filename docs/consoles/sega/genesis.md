# Genesis / Mega Drive

*1988–1997* · Outputs RGB stock, but the Model 2 has jailbars and the audio varies by board. Also covers the Sega CD, 32X and Nomad.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **Model 1** | VA0 to VA7, best audio on early boards |
| **Model 2** | VA0 to VA4, jailbars common |
| **Model 3** | Majesco budget model, few mods |
| **Nomad** | Portable Genesis |

## Video

### Triple Bypass (3BP)

Model 2: bypasses the stock video encoder. Sharp RGB with no jailbars, plus cleaner composite and S-Video.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Night-and-day audio on Model 2 and 3, plus cleaner RGB. Overkill on a Model 1 or a late VA4 Model 2. The standard install disables composite, and the video chip pins are fragile.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.
</div>
