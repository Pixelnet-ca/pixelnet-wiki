# Master System

*1986–1992* · The first model outputs RGB stock; the Master System II lost its A/V port.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **SMS1** | Has RGB on the A/V port |
| **SMS2** | RF only |

**Legend:** :material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it** · :material-book-open-variant: **Guide** on this wiki · :material-power-plug: **No install** (cart, adapter or software)

## Video

### AV / RGB mod (SMS2)

Brings back composite and RGB on the Master System II.

## Audio

### FM sound unit

Adds the Japanese FM sound chip (YM2413) for the richer soundtrack of supported games.

## Games & storage

### Master EverDrive

Flash cartridge for Master System games.

:material-power-plug: No install

## Region & frequency

### Region and 50/60 Hz switch

Switches between Japanese and export mode, and between 50 and 60 Hz.

## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap

Replaces the old electrolytic capacitors. Fixes or prevents hum, weak sound, dim picture and power problems.
