# Master System

*1986–1992* · The first model outputs RGB stock; the Master System II lost its A/V port.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **SMS1** | Has RGB on the A/V port |
| **SMS2** | RF only |

## Audio

### SMSFM

FM sound expansion for the SMS1 and SMS2: adds the Japanese FM sound chip (YM2413) for the richer soundtrack of supported games.

:material-account-hard-hat: by [Tim Worthington](https://etim.net.au){ target=_blank } · [:material-cart: Buy](https://etim.net.au/shop/shop.php){ target=_blank }

## Region & frequency

### Region and 50/60 Hz switch

Switches between Japanese and export mode, and between 50 and 60 Hz.

## Maintenance & reliability

### Recap

Replaces the old electrolytic capacitors. Fixes or prevents hum, weak sound, dim picture and power problems.
