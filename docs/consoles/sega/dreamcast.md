# Dreamcast

*1998–2001* · Outputs VGA (480p) stock. An ODE replaces the noisy drive, the most common failure.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **HKT-3000 / 3010 / 3020** | Region versions; mods are the same |

## Video

### Retro G.E.M.

Internal HDMI kit that taps the console's digital video. Basic version (720p, great with an external scaler) or Shiny version (full built-in scaling). With a PixelFX Morph 4K, adds Game-ID and per-game settings.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r4">★★★★☆</span> **Recommended** · A strong alternative to the DCHDMI, from the same digital source. Fewer long-term reports so far.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d3">■■■□</span> **Advanced** · Fine soldering on small points, removing parts, cutting traces.
</div>

### DCHDMI

Internal digital HDMI output, open source. Cleaner than a VGA-to-HDMI adapter.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Reviews call it the best Dreamcast picture outside emulation, with the correct aspect ratio. Costly, and not an easy install.

## Games & storage

### GDEMU

Replaces the GD-ROM drive: games load from an SD card, faster and silent.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Clones cost a fraction of a MODE and are what most people buy, with fast loading. Some owners raise concerns on early VA0 boards.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d1">■□□□</span> **Easy** · Screwdriver (often a game bit), drop-in parts, no soldering.
</div>

:material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it**

### MODE (Terraonion)

ODE with SD and SSD/HDD support and a richer menu.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d1">■□□□</span> **Easy** · Screwdriver (often a game bit), drop-in parts, no soldering.

## Upgrades & features

### Quiet fan

A quieter, cooler fan (often a Noctua) with a bracket. Great with an ODE.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r5">★★★★★</span> **Must-have** · Cheap and a big noise drop. A favourite next to an ODE.

## Power & battery

### DreamPSU

Modern replacement power supply: cooler and more reliable than the stock PSU.

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
<span class="pn-k">Community</span> <span class="pn-stars pn-r3">★★★☆☆</span> **Good, with caveats** · Runs much cooler, drop-in. But oscilloscope tests (Voultar) found it below the original PSU, and a cheap external adapter can cause problems.

<span class="pn-k">Install</span> <span class="pn-bar pn-d1">■□□□</span> **Easy** · Screwdriver (often a game bit), drop-in parts, no soldering.
</div>

### Clock battery

<div class="pn-rate" markdown>
