## Games & storage

### GDEMU

Replaces the GD-ROM drive: games load from an SD card, faster and silent.

:material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it**

### MODE (Terraonion)

ODE with SD and SSD/HDD support and a richer menu.

[:material-cart: Buy](https://www.terraonion.com){ target=_blank }

## Upgrades & features

### Quiet fan

A quieter, cooler fan (often a Noctua) with a bracket. Great with an ODE.

## Power & battery

### DreamPSU

Modern replacement power supply: cooler and more reliable than the stock PSU.

### Clock battery

Replaces the dead rechargeable clock battery (no more date prompt at every boot).
