# Xbox (original)

*2001–2006* · A very moddable console. First thing to do: remove the leaking clock capacitor.

| Model | Note |
|---|---|
| **1.0 to 1.6** | Motherboard revisions; mods depend on them |

**Legend:** :material-wrench: **PixelNet installs it** · :material-book-open-variant: **Guide** on this wiki · :material-power-plug: **No install** (cart, adapter or software)

## Video

### Internal HDMI

Internal board that adds a digital HDMI output.

## Games & storage

### Larger hard drive / SSD

Bigger IDE drive or SATA SSD with an adapter, for games and emulators.

## Softmod & homebrew

### Softmod

Installs a dashboard and homebrew without opening the console.

:material-power-plug: No install

### Modchip / TSOP flash

Hardware mod for a custom BIOS (e.g. OpenXenium, open source). TSOP flash works on revisions 1.0 to 1.5.

## Upgrades & features

### RAM / CPU upgrade

Advanced mods: 128 MB RAM and a faster CPU, useful for homebrew and emulators.

## Maintenance & reliability

### Clock capacitor removal (essential)

The clock capacitor leaks and eats the board. Remove it on every Xbox, modded or not.
