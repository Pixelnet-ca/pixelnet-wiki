# Sony

PlayStation, PlayStation 2 and PSP.

| Console | Years | |
|---|---|---|
| [**PlayStation**](playstation.md) | 1994–2006 | The disc drive is the weak point. An ODE replaces it and loads games from a microSD card. |
| [**PlayStation 2**](playstation-2.md) | 2000–2013 | Component video gives a great picture stock. Mods are mostly about loading games from a drive or SD card. |
| [**PSP**](psp.md) | 2004–2014 | A great handheld that's mostly modded through software. |
