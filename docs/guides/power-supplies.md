# Replacement power supplies

Original power bricks are often 30 to 40 years old. Most are unregulated: they put out much more than the voltage printed on them, run hot and wear out. A modern regulated switching supply is cooler, quieter and safer for your console.

## The 4 rules

1. **Voltage:** match the original (some consoles tolerate a little less, noted below).
2. **Current (A or mA):** equal or **higher** than the original. The console only draws what it needs.
3. **Polarity:** must match. **Wrong polarity can damage the console.**
4. **Plug:** same size and type. Never force a plug that doesn't fit.

!!! tip "Check before you plug in"
    With a multimeter, confirm the voltage and which contact is positive (tip or sleeve) before the first power-on.

## Our picks

The Triad Magnetics WSU series is the community's go-to: regulated, inexpensive, and available at [Mouser.ca](https://www.mouser.ca). In the part number, **-R** = centre negative, no -R = centre positive, **-13** = 5.5 × 2.5 mm plug (otherwise 5.5 × 2.1 mm).

### Internal power supply (figure-8 cord)

Saturn, PlayStation (original), PlayStation 2 (fat), Dreamcast and Xbox have their power supply inside. They only need a **C7 cord** ("figure-8"). North American consoles use the *polarized* version (one flat side, one rounded). Internal supplies can fail: if the console doesn't turn on, [contact us](mailto:support@pixelnet.ca).

### Handhelds

Game Boy, Game Boy Pocket / Color and Game Boy Advance run on batteries. The Game Boy Advance SP charges with its own plug (5 V USB cables are available). The PSP takes a 5 V DC EIAJ-02 plug or USB.
