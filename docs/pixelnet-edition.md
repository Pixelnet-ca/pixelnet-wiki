# PixelNet Edition

Fully restored and modded consoles, built one at a time in Trois-Rivières, Canada. Each one gets a number, a badge on the case and a build card in the box. This page is the official registry: if you own a PixelNet Edition, find your number below to see exactly what was done to it.

!!! info "How to check your console"
    Scan the QR code on your build card, or look for the red **PIXELNET EDITION #000** badge on the case and find that number here.
