# Glossary

Common terms from the world of retro consoles and mods, explained simply.

!!! tip "Tip"
    Use the search at the top of the page, or the table of contents on the right, to find a term quickly.

## Picture and video

### 240p
The native resolution of most consoles up to the PlayStation: a *progressive* 240-line picture. Many modern TVs mistake it for 480i and handle it badly, with blur and lag. A [scaler](#scaler) fixes that.

### Component (YPbPr)
A video signal split across three cables (green, blue, red). Quality very close to RGB. Available on PS2, GameCube and Wii.

### Composite
The yellow cable. The whole picture travels on one wire: it's the most common signal, but also the blurriest, with colours that bleed.

### CRT
A cathode-ray tube TV, the "big old TV". Retro consoles were designed for them: zero lag and the picture we're trying to get back.

### Csync
*Composite sync*. The recommended sync signal for RGB cables. It's cleaner than sync taken from composite video, which can cause interference.

### Input lag
The delay between pressing a button and seeing the action on screen. Modern TVs add a lot of it when processing old signals. A good scaler adds almost none.

### Jailbars
Thin vertical bars across the picture, mostly on the Genesis Model 2 and some SNES. They come from the console's video circuit and are fixed with a mod like the [Triple Bypass](#triple-bypass-3bp).

### Line doubling
A technique that doubles (or triples, quadruples…) each line of the picture to reach a resolution a modern TV accepts, with no noticeable lag. It's what the RetroTINK and OSSC do.

### RGB
The best-quality video signal a retro console can output: red, green and blue kept separate. Sharp picture, pure colours. Some consoles output it stock; others need an [RGB mod](#rgb-mod).

### S-Video
A step above composite: brightness and colour travel on separate wires. A noticeably clearer picture.

### Scaler
A device that sits between the console and the TV. It converts the analog signal into clean, sharp HDMI with almost no lag. Examples: RetroTINK, OSSC. See the guide [Which scaler should you use?](guides/scalers.md)

### SCART
The large rectangular European connector. It carries RGB, and it's the most common format for quality RGB cables.

## Maintenance and repair

### Capacitor
A small component that stores electricity. *Electrolytic* capacitors dry out or leak over the years: audio hum, unstable picture, a console that won't power on.

### Recap
Replacing a console's old [capacitors](#capacitor) with new ones. It's the basic maintenance that adds years to a console's life.

### Reflow
Redoing tired or cracked solder joints. It often fixes intermittent problems: sound cutting out, picture dropping.

### Retrobright
A technique to restore yellowed plastic to its original colour, using a peroxide-based product and UV light.

## Mods

### Audio amp
Replaces or bypasses the stock audio circuit for louder, cleaner sound. Examples: Mega Amp (Genesis), Game Boy amp.

### FreeMcBoot / OPL
A PS2 *softmod* installed on a memory card, without opening the console. OPL then lets you launch games from a hard drive or USB stick.

### HDMI mod
A digital output installed right inside the console. The sharpest possible picture, no analog cable. Examples: N64Digital, Hi-Def NES.

### IPS screen
A modern backlit screen that replaces a handheld's stock screen (Game Boy, Game Gear). Far brighter and easier to read.

### Lockout chip (CIC, 10NES)
A security chip that blocks games from other regions and causes the NES's famous blinking. A mod like the SuperCIC replaces or disables it.

### Modchip
A chip that lets a console read games from other regions or backup copies. Example: PSNee for PlayStation.

### Multi-out
Nintendo's proprietary video connector (SNES, N64). Added to an NES with an NESRGB, it lets you use SNES cables.

### ODE
*Optical Drive Emulator*. Replaces the disc drive with an SD card: faster loading, no more drive wear. Examples: XStation (PlayStation), GDEMU (Dreamcast), Rhea/Phoebe (Saturn).

### Region switch
A mod that lets you play games from other regions (Japan, Europe) on your console, or choose the language and speed (50/60 Hz).

### RGB mod
Adds an RGB output to a console that doesn't have one stock. Examples: NESRGB for NES, RGB mod for N64.

### Triple Bypass (3BP)
A Genesis Model 2 mod that bypasses the stock video circuit. Result: sharp RGB with no [jailbars](#jailbars), plus cleaner composite and S-Video.
